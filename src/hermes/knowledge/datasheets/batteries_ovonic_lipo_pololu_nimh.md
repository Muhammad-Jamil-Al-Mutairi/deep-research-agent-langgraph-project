## Voltage compatibility note

Components: OVO-3S-1400-50C, OVO-3S-2200-50C, OVO-3S-6000-80C, OVO-3S-7200-80C, POL-2226, POL-2225

A 3S LiPo pack is 11.1 V nominal and 12.6 V at 4.2 V per cell, close to the 12 V rating of the 12 V gearmotors. The NiMH packs (7.2 V and 8.4 V) run 12 V gearmotors at reduced speed, because brushed-motor no-load speed scales approximately with supply voltage.
